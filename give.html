Kingdom Impact

Sponsorship & Partnership Opportunities

At WMW International, we believe that every seed sown produces eternal fruit. Your sponsorship, prayers, and partnership make it possible to build churches, host international crusades, and empower women and ministries around the world.

Become a Sponsor of the WMW Conference

Join hands with us as we gather women from every walk of life for the annual WMW International Conference—a time of teaching, impartation, worship, and revival.

Your sponsorship helps us:

  • Provide conference materials, scholarships, and resources for attendees.
  • Support women in ministry who are being trained and equipped for leadership.
  • Expand our reach through global streaming and media production.

Every contribution makes a Kingdom impact.

Help Build Churches Around the World

WMW International is in the business of building churches! Through our partnerships and missions initiatives, we are helping to establish churches, prayer centers, and ministry hubs in communities that are hungry for the Gospel.

You can sponsor a church directly, give a general donation, or contribute to building materials, outreach programs, and ministry development.

Your giving helps turn vision into reality—creating places where lives are changed and communities are transformed.

✈️ Sponsor an International Crusade

As the world grows darker, we are committed to bringing the light of Jesus Christ into the deepest corners and forgotten places of the earth. Your sponsorship fuels our international crusades, which bring healing, deliverance, and salvation to nations across the globe.

Every dollar given goes toward travel and logistics for ministry teams, crusade setup and outreach materials, local partnerships and follow-up discipleship efforts. No gift is too small—every amount helps us reach one more soul.

Become a Monthly Partner

When you become a Monthly Ministry Partner, you join a family of believers committed to spreading revival and restoration worldwide. Your monthly partnership—through prayer and financial support—extends the reach of the Kingdom by funding Conferences, MasterClasses, Global Crusades, Church Plants, and Media Outreach.

We thank you in advance for standing with us—your consistency fuels our ability to do what God has called us to do.

“For God is not unjust to forget your work and labor of love which you have shown toward His name…” — Hebrews 6:10, NKJV
