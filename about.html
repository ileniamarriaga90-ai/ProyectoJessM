Founder & Director, WMW International

Dr. Jessica M. Marquez

Early Life & Calling

Early Ministry & Preparation

Dr. Jessica M. Marquez began active ministry at the age of ten, serving as a Sunday School teacher and musician in the local church pastored by her father. At 13, she became the youth leader of her church, demonstrating early leadership and a strong commitment to ministry.

During her time at Texas Bible College, she met Antonio Marquez, the son of missionaries Alex Marquez (serving as Vice President of the United Pentecostal Church of Mexico) and Henrietta Marquez.

Two weeks after their marriage, the couple relocated to Guadalajara, Jalisco, Mexico. They served two years as Associates in Missions (AIM) before being appointed by the Global Missions Board as full missionaries—becoming the youngest appointed missionaries at that time (Antonio was 20 and Jessica 21).

10+ Years

International Missionary Service

The Marquezes served in Mexico for almost 12 years. During this time, Jessica Marquez held several leadership positions, including Michoacán District Ladies’ President and personal secretary to the National Ladies’ President of Mexico.

In 2007, they were appointed as missionaries to Costa Rica, where Jessica functioned as national coordinator for the World Network of Prayer, active in women's ministry and teaching at the national Bible college in San José.

In 2009, they were appointed to the Dominican Republic, residing in Santiago. They oversaw three regions and three Bible colleges. Throughout these years, Jessica also assisted in establishing the Caribbean and Central America Regional World Network of Prayer.

2012

North American Ministry

Jessica has ministered extensively around the world, preaching and teaching in North America, Central America, the Caribbean, South America, Australia, Europe, and Asia across forty-two countries. Her focus remains on discipleship, leadership development, and mentoring women in ministry.

In January 2012, after nearly seventeen years of missionary service, Antonio and Jessica transitioned to pastoral ministry in Miami, Florida. In 2017, Jessica became the first woman ordained in the Florida District of the UPCI.

During that same year, she planted a church in downtown Miami. Over the course of their ministry, the Marquezes have helped establish seventeen churches in four different countries, all of which remain active today.

Global Movement

Women Ministering to Women International

Dr. Jessica is the founder and director of Women Ministering to Women International (WMW International), a global leadership and conference movement where gatherings have exceeded 9,000 attendees and tens of thousands have received spiritual impact.

She also founded an online network for pastors' wives connecting over 1,800 leaders, and reaches an audience of over three million through her digital media platforms. She holds an Ordination License with the UPCI and advanced theological studies at Urshan Theology and Southwestern Baptist Theological Seminary.

In 2017, she was ratified as Southeast Regional Director for Building the Bridge (serving 8 states), and in 2019 became the first female and Spanish-speaking chaplain of the Liberty County Sheriff’s Department.

Credentials & Public Service

Leadership, Education, and Service

She has served as chaplain for the Pulaski County Sheriff’s Department and the Little Rock Police Department in Arkansas. She is an active member of the International Conference of Police Chaplains and trained in Critical Incident Stress Management.

In recognition of her community leadership and work with law enforcement agencies, she was nominated and approved as a biographical candidate for Who’s Who in America.

In March 2026, both Jessica and Antonio were formally granted the right to bear and use the title “Doctor of Divinity” (honoris causa), recognizing their decades of ecclesiastical and theological leadership.

Civic Impact & Legacy

Civic Engagement & Family Legacy

Jessica and Antonio Marquez continue to travel internationally preaching, teaching, and instructing Master Classes throughout the year with a focus on global evangelism and leadership cohorts.

Above all professional accomplishments, Jessica considers her greatest blessing to be her family. She and her husband Antonio have been married for twenty-eight years.

They are the proud parents of Antonio Jr., Lexy, and son-in-love Joey, and the joyful grandparents of their grandson, Ollie.
