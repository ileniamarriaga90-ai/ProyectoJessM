The Heart Behind the Mission

God is opening doors across the globe

God has opened doors I could never have imagined. Through those doors, I have met women with a profound hunger for God and a deep desire to serve, yet so many have limited access to training, resources, and encouragement.

That is the heart behind Women Ministering to Women International. We exist to restore, renew, and equip women to fulfill their God-given purpose and calling.

When a woman is equipped, the impact reaches far beyond her own life. Her family, church, and community are transformed through her obedience to God.

— WMW International

A Personal Word

From Dr. Jessica M. Marquez

I have seen what God can do when someone receives encouragement, sound teaching, and an opportunity to grow. I have watched women find their voice again, renew their passion for ministry, and step forward in faith.

This work is deeply personal to me. I love God, and I love His people. My desire is to see women grounded in truth, confident in their calling, and equipped to serve wherever He sends them.

Thank you for praying, giving, and believing with us. Your partnership means so much to me and to the women we serve. There are still women to reach, lives to strengthen, and nations waiting to hear the Gospel.

Keep readingShow less

Somewhere today, someone is asking God if He can still use them. A leader is pouring into others while needing someone to pour into them. A family is searching for hope. A community is waiting to hear the Gospel.

These are the people I carry in my heart.

Throughout my years in ministry, I have seen what happens when someone receives encouragement, biblical teaching, and an opportunity to answer the call of God. Renewed faith begins to influence a family. A person who discovers their purpose begins to strengthen their church. Someone who once felt overlooked begins to minister to others.

This is the heart of WMW International.

WMW International carries a name that reflects our founding vision. The reach of our ministry extends to men, women, young people, and families across nations and generations.

Our mission is to restore, renew, and equip people to walk confidently in their God given passion, purpose, calling, and ministry. Our commitment is rooted in Apostolic truth, sustained through prayer, and focused on reaching people with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

I invite you to become part of this work. Help us prepare people to serve faithfully wherever God has placed them and carry His Word into the places He opens before us.

Dr. Jessica M. Marquez Founder & Director, WMW International
