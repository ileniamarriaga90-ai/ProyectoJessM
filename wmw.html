Let Us Partner with You… (5 Steps)

Partnership with WMW International and VIDA International Ministries is more than a connection—it’s a covenant relationship rooted in faith, prayer, and shared Kingdom purpose. Together, we can reach nations, equip women, and release a worldwide harvest of revival.

Step 1 — Pray: Begin by seeking the Lord’s direction regarding partnering with WMW International and/or VIDA International Ministries.
